01 / Meet your nanny

A steady, caring presence for your family.

Little routines. Growing confidence. A familiar, caring face.

Rose is a background-checked caregiver with many years of experience caring for children and supporting busy families.

Her approach is warm, practical, and professional, with safety at the center of every routine, activity, and handoff.

Her friendly, compassionate face is matched by attentive care for newborns, infants, toddlers, and school-age children up to 13 years old. Rose makes room for each child to feel safe, understood, and excited for the day ahead.

03 / How it works

Start with a conversation.

Every family has its own rhythm. Before care begins, talk through the details together.

  1. Share your family's needs.

    Tell Rose your child's age, the care you need, and your preferred dates. Use the calendar to find an appointment time.

  2. Talk through the details.

    Confirm fit, timing, location, and the current rate together. Share routines and anything Rose should know.

  3. Plan the day together.

    Agree on care and handoff details before the session, so you know what to expect.

Which areas does Rose serve?

How do I check appointment times?

Use any “Check availability” link to open Rose's Google Calendar appointment page and view available times.
